# 30-60-90-day roadmap

Finding IDs (F01 to F33) refer to `02-audit/findings.csv`. The outcomes are goals to work towards, not guarantees.

## Days 1–30: fix, launch, measure

| Week | Action | Findings | Outcome |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Security headers and caching on the current host | F01, F02 | Headers grade A on securityheaders.com |
| 1 | DMARC (`p=none` with reports), confirm DKIM, add CAA | F10 | Brand email spoofing becomes visible and can then be blocked |
| 1 | Google Search Console + Bing Webmaster; privacy-friendly analytics with enquiry goals | F15 | Baseline traffic and enquiry data from day one |
| 1 | Check the CDN/WAF rule that blocks testing tools | F14 | Google and monitoring tools can reach the site |
| 2 | Collect content: case studies, testimonials, team, prices or ranges, hours; logo decision | F22, F16 | Real proof ready for launch |
| 2–3 | Edit and review the new site; connect the form; legal review of the privacy notice | F20, F21 | Approved site ready |
| 3–4 | **Launch the new site**: redirects, sitemap submission, 404 and form tests | F03–F08, F11–F13, F17–F19, F28–F31 | 16 indexable pages; enquiry form live; mobile performance 95+ |
| 4 | First monthly report | — | Clear "before vs after" picture |

## Days 31–60: get found

| Action | Findings | Outcome |
|---|---|---|
| Claim and complete the Google Business Profile (categories, services, photos, hours) | F25 | Appears for local searches and Maps |
| Consistent listings on 5 directories (e.g. Clutch, GoodFirms, IndiaMART, JustDial, Startup India) | F26, F27 | Name, address and phone match everywhere |
| Publish the first 2 approved case studies and testimonials | F22, F32 | Proof on every service page |
| Complete LinkedIn and Instagram profiles (same name, logo, description, website link) | F24, F17 | A consistent brand across channels |
| Re-check DMARC reports, then move to `p=quarantine` | F10 | Spoofed email is blocked |
| Fix whatever Search Console reports (coverage, enhancements) | F28 | All pages indexed |

## Days 61–90: grow

| Action | Findings | Outcome |
|---|---|---|
| One practical article a month linked to a service (e.g. "When does a business need a custom ERP?") | F31, F32 | More search entry points |
| Third case study; ask happy clients for Google reviews | F22, F26 | Proof and reviews build up |
| Review the analytics: which services get visits and enquiries; adjust copy and calls to action | F23, F33 | Decisions based on data |
| Positioning workshop (1 hour): separate paths for service buyers and research/talent partners | F33 | A clearer message for each audience |
| Quarterly Lighthouse, accessibility and security re-test | F01, F07, F18 | Standards maintained |

## How progress will be reported
Monthly: visits, enquiries (form, WhatsApp clicks, email clicks), top pages, search queries and positions from Search Console, uptime, issues fixed and next month's plan.
