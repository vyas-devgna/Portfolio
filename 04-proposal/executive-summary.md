## Where you are today

KT INDIA's website is carefully built, honest about what is in R&D, and already does several things well: correct titles and descriptions, structured data for your company and services, a privacy page, and a light page that loads quickly on desktop. The opportunity is to turn it from a research brochure into a site that **brings in and converts enquiries**.

A passive review on 3 October 2026 found 33 improvement points: 0 critical, 7 high, 14 medium and 12 low. The five that matter most:

| # | Finding | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **The whole company is on one page.** Search engines see two URLs (home and policies), so no individual service can rank on its own. | Buyers search for "ERP software Gujarat" or "PCB design company", not for a company name. |
| 2 | **No enquiry form.** The eight "Request service" buttons scroll to an email link. | Every extra step loses enquiries, and there is no record of who tried to get in touch. |
| 3 | **No published proof.** There are no case studies, testimonials or client names, yet the page states "05 major clients". | Buyers and partners look for evidence before they make contact. |
| 4 | **Mobile speed.** It scores 69/100 in lab tests (desktop 100), because heavy scripts and animation load on every visit. | Most first visits are on a phone. |
| 5 | **Hard to find and to measure.** There is no analytics, no verified Google Business Profile, no directory listings, only two social links, and no DMARC email protection. | You cannot improve what you cannot see, and the brand can be spoofed by email. |
