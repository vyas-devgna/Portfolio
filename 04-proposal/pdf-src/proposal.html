Top five findings

#FindingWhy it matters
1The whole company is on one page. Search engines see 2 URLs, so no service can rank on its own.Buyers search for the service, not the company name.
2No enquiry form. "Request service" buttons scroll to an email link.Every extra step loses enquiries, and none are tracked.
3No published proof. No case studies or testimonials, yet the page states "05 major clients".Buyers look for evidence before getting in touch.
4Slow on phones. Mobile 69/100, total blocking time 1.4 s (desktop 100).Most first visits are on a phone.
5Hard to find and to measure. No analytics, unverified Google profile, no listings, no DMARC.You cannot improve what you cannot see; email can be spoofed.

30-60-90-day roadmap

Fix and launch, get found, then grow.

DAYS 1–30

Fix, launch, measure

  • Security headers and caching
  • DMARC, DKIM check, CAA
  • Search Console, Bing, analytics with enquiry goals
  • Check the firewall rule that blocks testing tools
  • Collect case studies, testimonials, team
  • Launch the new site; submit the sitemap
  • First monthly report
Goal: enquiry form live; 16 pages indexable; mobile 95+.
DAYS 31–60

Get found

  • Complete the Google Business Profile
  • 5 consistent directory listings
  • Publish 2 approved case studies
  • Align LinkedIn and Instagram profiles
  • Move DMARC to quarantine
  • Fix Search Console issues
Goal: same name, address and phone everywhere; proof on every service page.
DAYS 61–90

Grow

  • One practical article per month tied to a service
  • Third case study; ask for Google reviews
  • Adjust copy based on analytics
  • 1-hour positioning session: buyers vs. research partners
  • Quarterly speed, accessibility and security re-test
Goal: decisions based on data; growing search entry points.

Monthly report includes

Visits · enquiries (form, WhatsApp, email clicks) · top pages · search queries and positions · uptime · issues fixed · next month's plan.
